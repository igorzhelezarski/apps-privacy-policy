How we think about privacy

The short version. Each game's own policy has the full details.

Your progress stays on your device

We don't run accounts or servers for our games. Saves and settings live on your phone or tablet.

Child-safe by default

When a young child is playing, ads are child-directed and never personalized, in line with COPPA and Google Play's Families policy.

You choose

Where the law requires it we ask before personalized ads, and you can change your mind in the game at any time.

We never sell your data

Payments go through the app store, and we never see your card details.
